Waste of Time

Back in 2022, a 17-year-old boy named Krish entered ABC University to pursue a B.Tech in Information Technology. Thanks to countless cinema scenes of colorful campuses and background music, he genuinely believed college life was going to be a 24/7 blockbuster film. Reality, of course, had other plans.

Fresh Brain to College

1st Year: Krish lived inside his self-made cinematic dreamland. Since the college treated freshers like delicate newborn babies, nobody troubled him too much. The entire first year slipped away as smoothly as butter on a hot tawa. Little did our hero know, the heat was slowly building up to roast him later.

2nd Year: The Burnt Butt

Still behaving like a carefree newborn baby, Krish assumed the next three years would glide by just as easily. But fate pulled a quick plot twist. The college suddenly turned on ultra-strict attendance tracking for every single class.

Our man Krish had developed a serious habit of bunking. His close buddy Ram—who belonged to a completely different department—was also a professional class-skipper. The big difference? Ram had master-level survival tactics to get his attendance marked before vanishing. Krish, on the other hand, had zero strategy—just pure, unfiltered bunking skills. Whenever Ram walked back into their room, there lay Krish: tucked comfortably into bed, earphones plugged in, happily binge-watching Game of Thrones.

Effects of the Mistake and Overcoming That

3rd Year: The cinematic dream completely shattered when the backlogs landed on his lap. Realizing his butt was officially on fire, Krish did a total U-turn. He turned into a model student, clocking nearly 98% attendance, learning every subject, and successfully wiping out one of his backlogs. He swore never to repeat that seasonal stupidity again.

What Happened in 4th Year

4th Year: Krish pulled off a solid comeback, clearing his final year project alongside his last remaining backlog, wrapping things up with an 80% score and 97% attendance. The boy had truly reformed. But right when the hero thought the happy ending had arrived, campus placement season walked in. The strict eligibility criteria locked the doors on him simply because of his old backlog history.

Black Tails on You

Krish proudly grabbed his B.Tech IT degree, but those old 2nd-year blunders ended up hanging behind him like a long, stubborn black tail during his job hunt. Having fun is great, but don't bunk your way into a corner—because while you might change into a reformed soul overnight, your academic record has a painfully long memory!
